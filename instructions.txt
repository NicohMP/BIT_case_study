AI Engineering Intern Case Study
Polymarket Signal Scanner
You're joining the data team at a tech-focused investment fund. Currently, our analysts and PMs
want to know if prediction markets are telling us anything useful about the stocks we own.
Polymarket is where people bet on real-world outcomes, including Fed decisions, tariffs, tech
regulation, and company milestones. These markets produce implied probabilities that might
contain signals for equity investors.
Your task
Build an application that scans prediction markets on Polymarket, figures out which ones are
relevant to public equities, and delivers the insights to analysts. The end result should be a
working system with a web interface, a real database and an LLM doing the heavy lifting on
analysis, which is summarized in automated reports. You have 2 weeks. You can use any AI
coding tools you want.
Narrow your Focus
Research about BIT Capital using publicly available information. including our website, fund
documents, press, LinkedIn: whatever you can find. Understand what we invest in, which
sectors we focus on, and what our current holdings look like.
Market data ingestion
Pull active markets from Polymarket using their public API. Parse the data and extract what
matters: market question, current probability/price, volume, category, expiry date. This should
run as a scheduled pipeline, not a one-off script.
Database
Store everything in Postgres database. We recommend using Supabase but you are free to
choose. Design your schema to capture markets, filtered signals, and generated reports. We'll
look at how you think about normalization, relationships, and whether the data is actually
queryable.
Intelligent filtering
This is the most important part. Most Polymarket markets have nothing to do with equities.
You need to filter down to the ones that actually matter. This includes things like tariff outcomes,
rate decisions, tech regulation, company-specific events, etc. We care a lot about how you filter
and what logic you use. Show us your judgment.
LLM-powered signal report
Use an LLM of your choice to generate a signal report for our analysts. The report should say
which prediction markets are most interesting right now, what they imply for specific stocks, and
why. We want to see how comfortable you are working with LLM APIs (as a hint, Gemini has a
generous free API tier). Local models are also allowed.
The report should have actual insight, rather than just summarizing the data.. We want to see
how you think about financial markets and what prompts you come up with.
